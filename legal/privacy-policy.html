Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11 September 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

WorkDaily is committed to protecting user privacy through an offline-first architecture. This Privacy Policy details how user information, local notifications, exported reports, and preferences are handled when using the WorkDaily Android application.

2. Information Entered & Stored

All information entered by you—including profile name, optional profile avatar photo, mobile phone number, daily wage configurations, attendance logs, overtime hours, advance payments, notes, and app preferences—is stored exclusively on your device inside Room SQLite database, DataStore preferences, and app-private internal storage.

WorkDaily operates without a remote backend server, user account requirement, or external cloud data transmission.

3. How Information is Used

Information entered into WorkDaily is used solely on your local device to:

  • Display your personal attendance calendar and passbook ledger
  • Calculate daily earnings, overtime pay, advance deductions, and net pending balances
  • Generate local PDF passbook reports when requested by you
  • Schedule local attendance reminders and daily/weekly earnings notifications on your device

4. Local Storage & Security

Because all data remains on your physical device in app-isolated private storage, your records are protected by Android system sandbox security. WorkDaily does not collect passwords, payment card details, or authentication credentials.

5. Third-Party Services & Network Activity

WorkDaily does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting SDKs, tracking frameworks, or cloud synchronization services. Internet network permissions are used solely when you explicitly initiate external contact actions (such as opening the WhatsApp channel, Instagram page, or launching an email client to contact support).

6. Local Notifications & Permissions

WorkDaily uses system AlarmManager and WorkManager to deliver local attendance reminders and summary alerts directly on your device. Notification content is generated 100% locally and may dynamically include user-configured wage rates or calculated work summaries. You can grant or revoke notification permissions at any time in Android Settings.

7. Data Retention, Deletion & User Control

Your information remains stored on your local device for as long as the application remains installed, or until you explicitly edit or delete individual records or clear application storage. Uninstalling WorkDaily permanently removes all internal database records and local profile assets. User-created backup ZIP files stored in downloads or external folders must be managed or deleted manually by the user.

8. Children's Privacy

WorkDaily is designed for working individuals, employees, supervisors, and contractors to manage daily wage records. WorkDaily does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Policy Changes & Support

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Material updates will be reflected in the Last Updated date at the top of this document.

Questions, Issues, or Support

If you experience a problem with WorkDaily, believe something is not working correctly, have questions about these Terms or our Privacy Policy, or need assistance with the application, you can contact the WorkDaily support team.
